# YA KNOW (The Unspoken Void)
### Original Heavy Metal Composition
**Genre:** Progressive Death Metal / Symphonic Blackened Djent
**Tuning:** 8-string guitar — Drop E (E B E A D G B E)
**Tempo:** Opens at 48 BPM (doom crawl) → explodes to 192 BPM → fluctuates with time sig changes
**Key:** D# Phrygian Dominant (the "flamenco of the dark")
**Time Signatures Used:** 7/8 → 4/4 → 13/8 → 6/4 → 5/4 → 3/4 → back to 7/8

---

## THE CONCEPT

"Ya know" — two throwaway words. You say them a hundred times a day without thinking.
But what if those two words are the sound of the universe choking on the things
it CANNOT explain? What if "ya know" is the gap between god and silence?
This song lives in that gap. It screams from inside it.

---

## SONG ARCHITECTURE

```
[INTRO — THE CRAWL]           48 BPM — 7/8 time
[VERSE I — THE FRACTURE]      192 BPM — 13/8 time
[PRE-CHORUS — THE TENSION]    192 BPM — 6/4 time
[CHORUS — THE DETONATION]     192 BPM — 4/4 time
[VERSE II — THE COLLAPSE]     144 BPM — 5/4 time
[PRE-CHORUS II]               192 BPM — 6/4 time
[CHORUS II]                   192 BPM — 4/4 time
[BRIDGE — THE VOID SPEAKS]    72 BPM — free time / rubato
[BREAKDOWN — OBLITERATION]    160 BPM — 3/4 polyrhythm over 4/4
[GUITAR SOLO — THE ORACLE]    192 BPM — no time signature (full chaos)
[FINAL CHORUS — RESURRECTION] 192 BPM — 4/4 time
[OUTRO — THE ECHO]            Back to 48 BPM — 7/8 time
```

---

## INSTRUMENTATION NOTES (For Studio / Band)

- **Guitar I (Rhythm):** 8-string in Drop E — muted djent chugs, tremolo picked runs,
  harmonic squeals every 4 bars to signal key changes
- **Guitar II (Harmony/Lead):** 7-string — counter-melodic lines, neo-classical sweeps,
  whammy-bar divebombs on every chorus exit
- **Bass:** 6-string fretless bass — follows the kick on breakdown, goes melodic on
  verse, slides through the void on bridge (almost inaudible, felt in the chest)
- **Drums:** Standard + second bass drum pedal on left + electronic triggers on snare
  (layered 808 sub-hit). Blast beats in verse, half-time groove on chorus,
  polyrhythmic "3 over 4" on breakdown — NEVER fully resolves until final chorus
- **Keys/Orchestra:** Pipe organ on intro, full string section on pre-chorus,
  choir ("Gregorian + death growl choir") on chorus, solo cello on bridge,
  synth arpeggio underneath everything at -18dB (always there, like a heartbeat)
- **Vocals:** Three simultaneous vocal textures —
  - *Clean tenor* (melodic, almost sad)
  - *Death growl* (subterranean, chest-rattling)
  - *Black metal shriek* (overhead, piercing — the voice of pure static)

### [GUITAR SOLO — THE ORACLE]
*(192 BPM but NO time signature. The soloist has total freedom.
This solo should tell a story. Here is the arc:)*

```
SECTION A — "THE QUESTION" (bars 1-8):
Neo-classical ascending run in D# Phrygian Dominant — think
Bach channeled through Dimmu Borgir. Precise. Almost academic.
Every note intentional. The solo asks: "What do we know?"

SECTION B — "THE FRACTURE" (bars 9-16):
Whammy bar chaos. Divebombs into silence and back.
Pinch harmonics that sound like screaming.
The solo loses the question. Gets lost. Panics.

SECTION C — "THE SEARCH" (bars 17-24):
Tapped arpeggios — right hand tapping melodic lines
while left hand holds sustained chords.
Two conversations at once. Searching.

SECTION D — "THE ANSWER" (bars 25-32):
Single notes. Pentatonic. Slow. Bends.
The most emotional, human section of the entire solo.
A melody so simple it hurts because everything before it was so complex.
The solo finds the thing that "ya know" is pointing at —
the thing that can't be named — and just... rests on it.
Three long, bent notes. Vibrato. Fade into the riff.
```

## PRODUCTION NOTES (For the Engineer / Producer)

- **Master tempo automation:** Program every BPM change as automation in the DAW —
  the human ear should feel these shifts like gear changes in a car, not clock-stops
- **The "heartbeat" synth:** A sine wave at 40Hz (sub-bass frequency, below hearing,
  FELT in the chest) runs under the ENTIRE song at -18dB. Never gets louder.
  Never stops. Even in the silences. This is the song's nervous system.
- **Silence is an instrument:** The song has 9 intentional silence moments.
  Each silence should be printed to the file as silence — no reverb tail fill,
  no ambient hiss. Pure digital zero. Make the listener lean in.
- **Choir tuning:** The Gregorian choir should be tuned 15 cents sharp of concert pitch —
  just enough to feel "wrong" to anyone who doesn't know why, but right to the gut
- **Guitar tone:** Three stages of gain. The intro has zero gain (clean, bell-like).
  Verse I has moderate gain. Breakdown is maximum saturation — compressed until
  the transients are gone and it sounds like a wall of bees made of iron
- **Mix the bass UP:** In heavy metal, the bass is often buried. Not here.
  The bass is a third voice. Mix it until you can hear the fretless slide clearly
- **The bridge:** The cello should be recorded in an empty church.
  No artificial reverb. Use the room.

## THE STATEMENT

Every heavy metal song is about something trying to exist
in a world that was not built for it.

This song is about the gap between what we know
and what we can say —
the space where "ya know" lives —
the place where language gives up
and something older than language
takes over.

That's where heavy metal lives too.

**YA KNOW.**
